Welcome to TOHIM

Every child deserves a family, an education, and a future.

Tower of Hope International Ministries is a non-profit working to restore the lives of orphaned children and to help pastors in Uganda fulfill their God-given calling. Through love, consistency, and community, we give children who have lost everything a real chance at a better life.

What began as a small act of compassion in 2005 continues to grow through education, healthcare, community support, and people who believe every child deserves hope.

TOHIM, since 2005

Tower of Hope International Ministries exists to empower and restore the lives of orphaned children, and to equip pastors to fulfill their calling through love and compassion so that every child in our care can have hope for a better future.

We've worked hand in hand with sponsors and partners across the world to help build a future for children in need, providing education, clean water, healthcare, and a stable community.

Some of our main activities

How we care for children in Uganda

Girl Child

Empowering the next generation of girls

We invest in the girls in our care through education and mentorship, raising a generation of young women with the confidence and tools to build a bright future for themselves.

Transformation Through Music

Music, dance, and drama as a way forward

We raise the children entrusted to our care through music, dance, and drama, creating a joyful outlet that keeps them engaged, expressive, and productive.

Education For All

Every child is entitled to an education

We've laid the groundwork to give the children in our care real access to school because education can open doors to opportunity and a different future.
